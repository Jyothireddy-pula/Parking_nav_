# Parking survey — quick instructions

*(Print this page and bring it with you. No technical background needed.)*

## What you're doing

Every 5 minutes, at your assigned lot or gate, you write down what you
actually see. Nothing is estimated or rounded off.

## Before your first shift at a lot

- [ ] Walk the whole lot once and count the painted parking spaces
      (not cars — empty spaces count too). This is the lot's **capacity**.
- [ ] Write the capacity number on your sheet before you start counting
      occupied spaces.
- [ ] Look for one good spot where a camera could see most of the lot
      later. Jot down where it is and what it can't see.

## Every 5 minutes, at a parking lot

Write one line with:

| Time | Your name | Lot ID | Capacity | Cars parked right now | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Count what you see right now.** Don't guess "about 30" — walk the row
  if you're not sure.
- If something's odd (an event happening, a section blocked off), write
  it in Notes.

## Every 5 minutes, at a gate

Write one line with:

| Time | Your name | Gate ID | Cars in | Cars out | Queue (if any) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- "Cars in/out" = since your last line, not the whole day.
- Queue length is a rough estimate — that's fine, just don't call it exact.

## If you're one of two observers at the same lot at the same time

Count **independently**. Do not look at each other's sheet or discuss
numbers until both of you are done for that time slot. This is
intentional — it's how we measure how much two careful people naturally
disagree, so don't try to "agree" on a number.

## When you're done

- Hand in your sheet exactly as written. Don't cross things out or
  "clean it up" — if you made a mistake, write a note next to it instead.
- Make sure your sheet has the session name/number written at the top
  (from your schedule) so it can be matched with any other observer at
  the same lot.

## Rules of thumb

- **See it, write it. Don't see it, don't write it.** No guessing to fill
  a blank cell — leave it blank and note why.
- **Whole numbers only** for capacity and occupied spaces — actual counts,
  not "roughly."
- **When in doubt, write a note.** A note is never wrong; a guessed number
  can be.

Questions? Ask your survey coordinator before your shift, not mid-count.
